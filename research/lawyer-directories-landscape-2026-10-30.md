# Lawyer directory landscape (researched Oct 30, 2026)

## How many
- A third-party index lists **151 lawyer directory sites in the US**: https://atlas.navi-index.com/category/legal
- The realistic count is roughly 150 to 200 or more if you add state and local bar referral services, all 50 state bar license lookups, and peer-ranking guides (Chambers, Legal 500, Best Lawyers). This is an estimate.
- **The market is concentrated.** Internet Brands (owned by KKR) runs Martindale-Avvo: Avvo, Martindale-Hubbell, Lawyers.com, Nolo, AllLaw, Ngage, Captorra, and FindLaw since Dec 2024. It claims "over 25 million consumers monthly": https://www.martindale-avvo.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Martindale-Avvo-AI-Shapes-Legal-Search-Uncovering-the-True-Value-of-Trust-1.pdf

## Success vs. failure pattern
- **Still doing well:** sites with SEO built on content or free law (Justia, Nolo, FindLaw) and badge or peer-rating businesses (Super Lawyers, Martindale). They make money from flat subscriptions, ads, and websites they build for law firms.
- **Shut down:** products that took a cut of fees or acted as referral services (Avvo Legal Services 2016 to 2018; UpCounsel's first run, closed in 2020).
- **Under pressure:** AI search answers cut click-through (Martindale-Avvo cites 50 to 80% CTR drops where AI summaries appear, in a similar category). Lenders are wary, and Google's own profiles and ads take local intent.

## What this means for LegalGrads
- **The general directory space is crowded and consolidated.** Most new CA admits already have auto-created Avvo, Justia, and Lawyers.com profiles, and the State Bar search covers license status.
- **The new-admit angle is open but narrow.** No major player is built around new or recent admits, and none offers flat-fee "first clients" pricing for young lawyers. The weak spot is that new admits have small budgets, and consumers rarely search for "new lawyer."
- **Ways to stand out:** fresher data than the auto-created profiles (Active only, admit date), in-person and nearby search for small businesses, and flat, clearly labeled paid upgrades with no fee splitting.
- **Biggest risk:** getting traffic. Justia and Avvo dominate the same name and city searches. City or practice pages need unique content to rank.
